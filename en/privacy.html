Privacy policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Solitaire collects no personal data, shows no ads and uses no analytics or tracking tools.

What stays on your device

Games in progress, statistics, daily challenge history and settings are stored only on your device. None of it is sent to any server, and all of it is deleted when you remove the app.

Game Center

If you sign in to Game Center, the scores, times and streaks sent to the leaderboards are kept by Apple under the Game Center privacy policy. Without Game Center the game works the same and nothing is sent.

Tips

Tips are paid through the App Store. The app never receives or stores payment details.

Contact

Privacy questions can be sent through the support page.
