Frequently asked questions

Does every deal really have a solution?

Yes, in both Draw 1 and Draw 3. Before dealing, the app shuffles and tries to solve the game. If it can't, it shuffles again. Hints and the wand follow that same solution.

Why didn't my win count toward my records?

Using a hint or the wand makes the game assisted. It still counts as played, but it stays out of records, streaks and leaderboards. Undo costs points but doesn't make a game assisted.

Where are my statistics stored?

Only on your device. They're never sent to a server, and they're deleted if you remove the app. Game Center leaderboards are kept by Apple.

When does the daily challenge change?

At midnight Brasília time (UTC−3). Everyone gets the same deal, and only your first attempt counts toward the calendar and the leaderboard.
